Privacy Policy for Caval

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Caval, an offline knight's tour puzzle game, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Caval does not require an account. The app is designed to work offline and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The app may store game progress, completed puzzles, best results, and preferences locally on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to provide game features, save progress, remember preferences, and display results. Caval does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Internet Access and Third Parties

Caval is designed to work completely offline and contains no advertisements. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection, and gameplay data is not intended to be transmitted to a server.

Your device operating system and app store may independently collect information according to their own privacy policies. If the app uses analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services, this policy must be updated to explain their data practices.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Game progress and preferences are stored locally if the app's features save them. This information may remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Removing app data may permanently delete your saved progress.

5. Permissions

Caval is intended to use only the device capabilities necessary for gameplay. You can review and manage app permissions in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Caval does not require users to submit personal information to play. The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. Please contact the developer if you have a privacy concern related to the app.

7. Security

Keeping game data on your device reduces the need to transmit it. However, no storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device using its available security features.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using Caval at any time. You can usually remove locally stored app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the website where this policy is hosted.
